Students from the Napa Campus can choose to purchase a General Admission Ticket, or an All Inclusive Ticket. General Admission ticket does not include transportation to and from Napa Campus, or lodging.

All Inclusive Ticket Includes:

  • Transportation to and from Conference
  • Lodging during Conference
  • Dinner on Thursday night
  • Entry to all days of Conference
The All Inclusive ticket is only available to junior highers and high schoolers at the Napa Campus. Young Adults must purchase a General Admission ticket.
