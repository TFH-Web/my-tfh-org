Discover

Over two weeks, our purpose is to help you discover Life at The Father’s House and what that looks like for you personally. God has given our church a powerful story and big vision. We want to help you connect with where we’ve been and where we’re going, as well as to meet new friends and learn more about your purpose and where that has a place here at The Father’s House.

Our hope is that after going through both discover gatherings you would find family within The Father’s House community and you would make the decision to call TFH your home church! We believe you will discover your God-given purpose and be given practical steps on how to live your best life.

For more info, visit here: tfh.org/discover
