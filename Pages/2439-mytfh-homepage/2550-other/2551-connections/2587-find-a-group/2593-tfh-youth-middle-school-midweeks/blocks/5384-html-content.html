TFH YOUTH | MIDDLE SCHOOL MIDWEEK

Campus: Vacaville | Category: Youth (Jr. High & High School)


GROUP DETAILS

Every Wednesday besides Rally, we meet at the Vacaville Campus in Building C from 7 to 8:30 PM. This is a safe environment where we have fun, spend some time in worship, hear a short word, and then split into small groups on campus. We have seen so many kids encounter the love of Jesus, build friendships, and grow in their faith, and we would love to invite your student to join us!
