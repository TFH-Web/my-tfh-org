Adult Co-Ed | Mike & Anita Morse

Vacaville Campus | Adult Co-Ed Group
Group Topic | Bible Study, Curriculum
Group Leader(s) | Anita Morse & Mike Morse
Description: 18 and up welcome! Come get Unstuck with us!Many feel stuck in the routine of life—their jobs, relationships, and daily circumstances. They know it's not the life they dreamed of. And they feel stuck in a life yearning for deeper meaning. The Unstuck Bible study has been designed to help guide you out of the ruts and pitfalls that so often ensnare us all. You will be inspired and equipped by teaching from Mark Batterson, Francis Chan, Lisa Harper and Rich Stearns.

Our study this semester will be based on Dr. David Jeremiah's "Count it All Joy" series. Workbooks are available, but not mandatory.
