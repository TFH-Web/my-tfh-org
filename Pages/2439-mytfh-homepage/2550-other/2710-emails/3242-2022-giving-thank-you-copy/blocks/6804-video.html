Dear Parents/Guardians,

We would like to take this opportunity to communicate with you about all the fun and exciting things

your kids will be learning and participating in over the next 7 weeks. As you are aware we are currently

launching a building Campaign called BEYOND, for the purpose of creating a place “Where people far

from God can find life in Christ”.


We believe that as you go through YOUR journey to discover how God can use you in this process, that

God will also be taking your kids on their own journey. These two journeys are meant to intertwine with

each other. We would like to partner with you in helping guide your family through this process.

Below you will find a detailed schedule of the lessons and resources your kids will receive during their

time in church each weekend. Our hope is that you will partner with us in utilizing these recourses to

better help your child understand that God has a big role for them to play in building His house.

Schedule:

• Sunday, March 12th – Beyond Series: Vision

• Sunday, March 19th – Beyond Series: Faith

• Sunday, March 26th – Beyond Series: Prayer

• Sunday, April 2nd – Beyond Series: Surrender

• Sunday, April 9th – Easter Sunday

• Sunday, April 16th - Beyond Series: Give

• Sunday, April 23rd – Beyond Series: Commit

• Saturday, April 29thth – Family Event: Color Run Jog-A-Thon

• Sunday, May 7th – Color Run Sponsor Money Due

Things to know:

• Journey Guides: Your kids have also received their very own journey guide in the form of a

treasure map. They should bring these to church with them every weekend to earn a new

piece of the map. They will be following along on a journey with Minnesota Mandy, our local

archeologist, to discover the most amazing treasure of all! This journey works best if they don’t

miss a week.

• Family Talk: On the back of your kids journey guides is something we call “Family talk”. This is

where your journeys collide. These simple activities were designed to allow You, the parent, to

lead your child through their journey in a fun way. They probably won’t even realize they are

learning God’s word!

• YouTube: During “Family Talk” you will notice that we reference Minnesota Mandy’s YouTube

page. All of the Minnesota Mandy Adventures will be available to view each week on our TFH

Kidmin Youtube Page under the “Minnesota Mandy” Playlist. Check it out and don’t forget to

subscribe or comment to get a reply from Minnesota Mandy herself.

• Tag Us: We want to celebrate your child’s journey! When posting anything your child does for

the BEYOND Campaign make sure to tag @tfhkidmin and we will display their photo in their

classroom on the weekend.

• Color Run / Jog-A-Thon: One of the ways your child can participate in raising money for our new

building is to participate in our Color Run. Your adventurer will collect pledges from family

members and friends on how many laps they think they can run. You can collect more

information about our color run at the welcome desk in any of our kids buildings
