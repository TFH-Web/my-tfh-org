Lunch Bunch Registration

Lunch Bunch is a fun, after-school option where preschoolers bring their own lunch from home and enjoy eating together, playing on the playground, listening to a story, and joining in a special activity planned by Ms. Arah. It’s a small group setting that gives kids extra connection time—and parents an extra hour before pickup!

Which day do I select?

Select one of the options below based on how many days a week your child currently attends:

  • 2-days: Select Thursday
  • 3-days: Select Wednesday
  • 5-days: Select Wednesady or Thursday (only register for one)
