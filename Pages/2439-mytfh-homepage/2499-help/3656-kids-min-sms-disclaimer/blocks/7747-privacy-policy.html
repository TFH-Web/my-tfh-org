Auto Enrollment Policy for SMS Messaging
To prioritize the safety and security of children in our care, all phone numbers used during the child check-in process are automatically enrolled to receive SMS text messages. These messages are intended solely for operational and safety-related communication, such as check-in confirmations, emergency alerts, or pickup instructions. They are not used for marketing or promotional purposes. By using your phone number for check-in, you acknowledge and consent to this form of communication.
